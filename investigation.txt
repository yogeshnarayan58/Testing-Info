AC1: Results Dashboard Availability
Given a survey has been successfully published
When publication completes
Then the Survey Results dashboard shall become available immediately to authorized users
And the Survey Results step shall be highlighted in the survey workflow tracker.

AC2: Post-Publish Navigation
Given a user publishes a survey
When publication completes
Then the user shall be directed to the Find My Stuff (FMS) experience
And a link to the Results Dashboard shall be visible and selectable
And a message shall inform the user that their survey has been published and results can be monitored from the dashboard.

AC3: Stage 1 – No Responses Received
Given a survey has been published and no responses have been received
When a user accesses the Results Dashboard
Then a disclaimer shall indicate that the survey is newly launched and insufficient data has been collected
And charts and tables shall be displayed in an empty state
And KPI components shall be visible and active
And the Survey Overview tab shall be available
And Export and Share functions shall be disabled.

AC4: Stage 2 – Responses In Progress
Given a survey has received responses but has not reached its target completion threshold
When a user accesses the Results Dashboard
Then a disclaimer shall indicate that results are still being collected
And charts and tables shall display currently available responses
And KPI values shall accurately reflect collected responses
And the Survey Overview tab shall remain available
And Export and Share functions shall remain disabled.

AC5: Stage 3 – Survey Complete
Given a survey has achieved its required response target or completed fielding status
When a user accesses the Results Dashboard
Then the disclaimer shall be removed
And Export and Share functionality shall be enabled
And the AI Summary shall become available
And the Survey Overview tab shall remain available.

AC6: Dashboard State Management
Given a survey transitions between response collection states
When response volumes increase or completion criteria are met
Then the dashboard shall automatically reflect the appropriate stage (No Responses, In Progress, Complete) based on survey status and response thresholds.

AC7: KPI Display
Given a user accesses the Results Dashboard
When KPI data is available
Then the following KPIs shall be displayed in the specified order:

Total Responses
Completion Rate
Time Remaining on Survey
And KPI values shall reflect the most current survey data available.

AC8: KPI Refresh
Given new survey responses are received
When the dashboard is refreshed or reloaded
Then KPI metrics shall reflect the updated survey response counts and status.

AC9: Question-Level Visualization Support
Given survey results exist for a supported question type
When a user views the Results Dashboard
Then visualizations supported by the approved Chart Library shall be available for that question type.

shall be supported.

AC15: Survey Overview Access
Given a user accesses the Results Dashboard
When survey results are viewed
Then the Survey Overview tab shall remain accessible regardless of dashboard stage.

AC16: AI Summary Placement
Given a completed survey qualifies for AI Summary generation
When a user accesses the completed Results Dashboard
Then the AI Summary shall appear below the KPI section and above results visualizations.

AC17: AI Summary Generation
Given a survey has reached completion status
When the AI Summary is generated
Then the summary shall present:

Overall survey findings
Key trends
Notable insights
Significant conclusions
based on collected survey responses.

AC18: Export and Share Controls
Given a user has access to the Results Dashboard
When the survey is not yet complete
Then Export and Share controls shall remain unavailable.

And

When the survey reaches completion status
Then Export and Share controls shall become available according to the Export and Sharing requirements defined in dependent epics.

AC19: Empty-State Handling
Given dashboard visualizations do not yet contain data
When the user views a chart or table
Then the dashboard shall display an appropriate empty-state experience rather than an error.

AC20: Live Results Accuracy
Given responses are being collected
When the Results Dashboard is rendered
Then charts, tables, KPIs, and AI-generated insights shall be based on the latest available survey response data.

AC21: Authorization and Access Control
Given a user attempts to access a Results Dashboard
When permissions are evaluated
Then only authorized users shall be allowed to view survey results, exports, AI summaries, and related dashboard artifacts.

AC22: Design Compliance
Given the Results Dashboard is rendered
When users interact with the experience
Then layouts, component placement, workflow progression, and dashboard presentation shall align with the approved Survey Results dashboard designs.

Description
Background:

After users pass the survey "Build" portion of their user experience, they will "Review" their Survey and then send it through to "Publishing".  After the "Publishing" phase, the Survey will be fielded to participants and responses will begin to come through in a "Results" dashboard view for them to analyze. This epic is covering the UI build of the "Results" dashboard, inclusive of all the charts, tables, KPIs, AI Summary, and the placement of relevant functions. 

Goal: Develop the screens and functionality to support a "Results" dashboard where survey results are live and viewable by users in a variety of formats.  

 

Scope: 

Trigger
Dashboard should become available immediately upon publication.  When a user selects "Publish", they should unlock the dashboard view.  In the line tracker at the top of the screen "Survey Results" is highlighted.  There are different stages of the dashboard.
Just Published --> FMS
User should be immediately directed to Find My Stuff experience where the dashboard link is present, live, and selectable
Text disclaimer/pop-up required alerting the user that their survey was just published and can be referenced here. 
Stage 1 - No responses gathered
Disclaimer should be present on page with text reminder that the survey was just launched and the data is not robust
Share/Export functions should be disabled
Charts and tables are present, but empty
KPIs are present and live
Survey overview tab is present
Stage 2 - Survey responses coming in, not complete
Disclaimer should still be present
Share/Export functions should be disabled
Charts and tables are present, displaying incremental responses
KPIs are present and live, reflecting the increase in responses accurately
Survey overview tab is present
Notification should be sent out to user at the half-way point of full responses i.e. if user requires 400 responses, notification goes out at 200 responses (OOS for this epic, notifications will be handled in a separate JIRA, included for reference)
Stage 3 - Survey is complete, all responses gathered
Disclaimer is removed
Share/Export functions should be enabled
AI Summary unlocked
Survey overview tab is present
Notification should be sent to user that survey is complete (OOS for this epic, notifications will be handled in a separate JIRA, included for reference)
