Save question editor before waiting for preview
On a fresh Start from Scratch survey, filling the first two options leaves the initial editor open. question-type-selector.component.ts saves only on an outside click or Save changes. No saved preview row or Edit button exists yet, so this call times out for the three-option fixture and the remaining serial tests are skipped. Fill the extra options in the open editor, then explicitly save and wait for the preview question before returning.
